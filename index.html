About this app

Your location never leaves your device. This is a static web app with no backend: your browser reads your location (or the coordinates you type) and runs the whole search · distance maths, sorting, rendering · locally. No coordinates are sent to any server, and there are no cookies or tracking scripts.

Network traffic is one-way and minimal: the open <podcast:location> dataset is fetched from podcastindex.org and cached in your browser for 24 hours (see Data below), and cover images load directly from each podcast's own host. The globe view's 3D library and map outlines are served from this same site, not a CDN.

Some rows in the dataset name a place without coordinates (a bare "Springfield, USA" with no geo or OSM data). Those can't be plotted on a map or ranked by distance, so the counter above only includes podcasts the app can actually place.

A search is written into the page address so you can bookmark or share it · just know the link carries the coordinates you searched from.
